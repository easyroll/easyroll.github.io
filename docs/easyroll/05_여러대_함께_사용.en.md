# 4. Using Multiple Blinds Together

If you have installed several blinds, you can control them **all at once by zone (room)** and **line up their heights neatly**.

---

## Controlling a Whole Zone at Once

On the main screen, turning on the **[Whole zone]** toggle selects that entire zone, and the **[Select all]** toggle on the control panel selects every blind on the screen.

## Lining Up Heights Neatly — Auto-Level

When you **raise or lower several blinds together in one zone and then press [Stop]**, this feature **automatically lines up the heights** of the blinds that moved, using either the **highest one (when raising)** or the **lowest one (when lowering)** as the reference.

### ① Turn It On First (once per zone)

Tap the **⚙** next to the zone name → **[Zone device settings]** → **[Auto-Level]** → set the **zone you want to align to ON**

> 💡 The **[Auto-Level]** toggle on the main-screen control panel turns it on or off for **all zones at once**. (Turning it off also clears the settings you made for each zone.) To use it only in certain zones, turn it on zone by zone under **[Zone device settings] → [Auto-Level]** above.

> ℹ️ This setting is saved **on this phone (app) only**. Family members or other phones need to turn it on separately.

### ② Using It

In a zone where Auto-Level is on, after moving **two or more blinds together**:

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| **[Stop]** after **raising** | The rest line up to the **highest** one |
| **[Stop]** after **lowering** | They line up to the **lowest** one |
| **Press and hold [Stop] for 1.5 seconds or longer** | An **[Align to top] · [Align to bottom] · [Align to average]** popup appears → they line up to the height you choose (highest · lowest · average height) |

> 💡 Automatic alignment runs when you **press [Stop] within 1 minute of pressing [Open] or [Close]**. (Moves made with M1–M3 or % are not auto-aligned.) If the blinds stopped on their own before you pressed [Stop] (for example, after a short press that moved them only a little), press **[Stop] once** within 1 minute. (If you have already pressed [Stop] once, or more than 1 minute has passed, another short press of [Stop] does nothing.)

> 💡 **Only the blinds that moved** are aligned. (For example, if you raise and stop only 2 of 4 blinds, only those 2 are lined up.)

> 💡 Even when the blinds are different lengths, they are aligned so that the **bottom bars actually end up level**.

> 💡 **To choose the alignment height yourself** — **keep holding [Stop] for 1.5 seconds or longer** without letting go, and an **[Align to top] · [Align to bottom] · [Align to average]** popup appears. Pick one and the selected blinds line up neatly at that height. You can use this at any time, even if you haven't just moved them — simply select the blinds you want to align first. (If you let go before 1.5 seconds, no popup appears and they align by movement direction as usual.)

> ℹ️ The popup appears **only when there is something to align** — that is, when you have selected **two or more blinds at different heights** in a zone where Auto-Level is on.

> ℹ️ Auto-Level works by **adjusting after the blinds stop**, so you may see a **slight correction movement** at the end right after stopping. This is normal.

## If a Blind Has a Weak WiFi Signal

A blind far from your router may respond slowly because of a weak signal.

- It helps to move the router **closer to the blinds**, or to add a **WiFi booster (extender)**.
- If just one blind is repeatedly slow or keeps dropping out, check the signal strength at that location.
