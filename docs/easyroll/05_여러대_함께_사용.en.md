# 4. Using Multiple Blinds Together

If you have installed several blinds, you can control them **all at once by zone (room)** and **line up their heights neatly**.

---

## Controlling a Whole Zone at Once

On the main screen, turning on the **[Whole Zone]** toggle selects that entire zone, and the **[Select All]** button on the control panel selects every blind on the screen.

## Lining Up Heights Neatly — Level Align

When you **raise or lower several blinds together in one zone and then stop**, this feature **automatically lines up the heights** of the blinds that moved, using either the **highest one (when raising)** or the **lowest one (when lowering)** as the reference.

### ① Turn It On First (once per zone)

Tap the **⚙** next to the zone name → **[Zone Device Settings]** → **[Level Align Setup]** → set the **zone you want to align to ON**

> 💡 You can also turn it on and off with the **[Level Align]** toggle on the main-screen control panel.

### ② Using It

In a zone where Level Align is on, moving **two or more blinds together** does the following:

| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Stop after **raising** | The rest line up to the **highest** one |
| Stop after **lowering** | They line up to the **lowest** one |
| **Press and hold the Stop button** | They line up to the **middle (average) height** |
| **Press [Stop] again while already stopped** | An **"Align to top / bottom"** popup appears → aligns to your choice |

> 💡 **Only the blinds that moved** are aligned. (For example, if you raise and stop only 2 of 4 blinds, only those 2 are lined up.)
> 💡 Even when the blinds are different lengths, they are aligned so that the **bottom bars actually end up level**.

> 💡 **To choose the align direction yourself** — while the blinds are stopped, press **[Stop] once more** to bring up an **[Align to top] · [Align to bottom]** popup. Pick a side and they line up that way.

> ℹ️ Level Align works by **adjusting after the blinds stop**, so you may see a **slight correction movement** at the end right after stopping. This is normal.

## If a Blind Has a Weak WiFi Signal

A blind far from your router may respond slowly because of a weak signal.

- It helps to move the router **closer to the blinds**, or to add a **WiFi booster (extender)**.
- If just one blind is repeatedly slow or keeps dropping out, check the signal strength at that location.
