# 3-1. Alarm Setup (Automatic at a Set Time)

You can have your blind **open and close automatically** at set times. (For example, open at 7 AM on weekdays.)

---

## Adding an Alarm

1. At the top of the app's main screen, tap **🕐** → **[Alarm]** → **[+]** at the top right
2. **Select a blind** (zone/device)
3. **Select a time**
4. **Select a blind position**
5. **Select the days** → **Save**

![Adding an alarm — select time, position, and days](../images/alarm_add.jpg){ width="300" }

| Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Time | What hour and minute it runs (or based on sunrise/sunset — see below) |
| Blind Position | **Top / Bottom / M1–M3 (saved positions) / a custom %** |
| Days | The days to repeat |
| Blind | Which zone/blind it applies to |

You can turn alarms you've created **on and off with a switch** in the list.

![Alarm list — time and sunrise alarms with on/off switches](../images/alarm_list.jpg){ width="300" }

---

## Match It to Sunrise / Sunset (Sunrise & Sunset Alarms)

Instead of a fixed time, you can also set it based on **sunrise (when the sun comes up) or sunset (when the sun goes down)**. It follows along automatically even as the times change with the seasons.

When adding an alarm, choose from **[Time | Sunrise | Sunset]** → then specify **[minutes before / minutes after]** the reference point.

> Example) Close **30 minutes after sunset** · Open **10 minutes before sunrise**

![Sunrise-based alarm — specifying minutes before/after](../images/alarm_sun.jpg){ width="300" }

> 💡 It runs based on the sunrise/sunset times of your region (the region you selected at sign-up).

---

## Editing & Deleting Alarms

- **Turn on/off** — the switch next to the alarm in the list
- **Edit** — select the alarm in the list → change its details
- **Delete** — **press and hold the alarm card** and a delete screen appears. Select the alarm to remove and delete it.

---

> ⚠️ **Alarms require an internet connection.** At the set time, the **server sends the command to the blind**, so the blind must be connected to the internet (home WiFi) for it to work.

> 💡 Alarms work even when your phone is turned off (because the server sends them). However, if the blind is offline, it may miss the action at that time.
