## Before Assembly, Check That the Motor Works

**Before** installing it into the blind, do a quick check that the motor unit runs properly.

1. Connect the **adapter (power connection cable)** to the motor unit.
2. **Plug the adapter into an outlet.**
3. If the motor **moves back and forth (up and down) a little and then stops → it's working!**

👉 When you connect power, the motor makes a **tiny rotation in both directions**. This isn't a fault — it's a **"working properly" greeting motion**.

!!! success "If you see this motion, the motor itself is fine"

    If registration or control doesn't work afterward, it's likely not a motor fault but a **Wi-Fi / communication issue**. See the [Troubleshooting FAQ](06_문제해결_FAQ.md).

- **Once confirmed, unplug the power** and move on to [1-1. Installation Guide (Assembly)](install_guide.md).
- **If there's no response at all**, recheck the adapter and cable connections, and if it still doesn't work, contact the [Customer Center](06_문제해결_FAQ.md#if-that-still-doesnt-solve-it).
