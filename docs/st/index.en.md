# SmartThings Integration

Connect your EasyRoll blind to **Samsung SmartThings** to control it alongside your Galaxy phone and Samsung appliances on one screen, and operate it with **Bixby voice** as well.

---

## What You'll Need

- An account with **your blind registered in the EasyRoll app** ([Register Device](../easyroll/02_앱_등록하기.md) completed)
- The **SmartThings app** installed on your smartphone + signed in with your Samsung account

## Connecting (One-Time Setup)

1. **SmartThings app** → **[+ Add Device]**
2. **[Partner devices (linked services)]** → search for **"EasyRoll"**
3. **Log in with your EasyRoll account** → allow the connection
4. When your registered blinds **appear in SmartThings automatically, you're done!**

> After connecting, when you move a blind, the position (%) on the SmartThings card **updates automatically along with it.**

## How You Can Use It

| Method | Example |
|---|---|
| **SmartThings card** | Open/close/stop, adjust position % |
| **Bixby voice** | "Hi Bixby, open the living room blind" / "Open the blind halfway" |
| **Automation (routines)** | Close the blinds in sleep mode, open them with your morning alarm, etc. |

## When Things Don't Work

| Symptom | Solution |
|---|---|
| EasyRoll doesn't appear in search | Update the SmartThings app to the latest version and try again |
| Connected but the device doesn't show | First check in the EasyRoll app that the blind is properly registered and online |
| Card controls don't respond | Check that the blind is online ([FAQ](../easyroll/06_문제해결_FAQ.md)) → disconnect the SmartThings link and reconnect |
