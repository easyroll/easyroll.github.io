# Delete your account and data

How to delete your account and related data in the **EasyRoll Smart Blind** app (developer: Inoshade Co., Ltd.).

## Delete in the app

1. Sign in to the EasyRoll app.
2. Tap **≡ (Settings)** at the top right of the home screen.
3. Tap **Change member information**.
4. Tap **Delete account** at the bottom and confirm.

Deletion takes effect **immediately** and cannot be undone. Signing up again with the same email starts a new account.

## If you cannot use the app

If you have removed the app or cannot sign in, contact us from the email address you signed up with and ask for **account deletion**. We will verify your identity, process the request and let you know.

- Email: inoshade@inoshade.co.kr

## Data that is deleted

Deleting your account **immediately** removes:

- Account information (email address, user ID, sign-in data, social sign-in links)
- Registered blind devices and zones
- Schedules (alarms)
- Family, colleague and temporary admin sharing links
- Device token used for app notifications

## Retention

- Records made before deletion may remain in **server operation logs** and **data backups**; these are deleted automatically within **30 days**.
- App usage statistics and error reports (Firebase) are kept in a form that does not identify you, under Google's policies.
- Settings stored on the blind device itself (such as Wi-Fi details) are cleared when the device is reset. Using **Delete device** in the app before deleting your account resets the device too.
