# Google Home Integration

Connect your EasyRoll Smart Blind to **Google Home** to operate it by voice with **"Hey Google"** on Google Nest speakers, Nest Hub displays and Android phones, and add it to **Routines** in the Google Home app so it moves automatically.

---

## Supported features

| Feature | In Google Home |
|---|---|
| **Open · Close** | Voice / Google Home app device card / Routines |
| **Any position (%)** | "Open the blind to 30%", "Open the blind halfway" — or use the slider (**100% = fully open**, Google convention) |
| **Current status** | When you open the Google Home app, it fetches the blind's current position and online status |
| **Routines** | Open in a "Good morning" routine, close in a bedtime routine, follow sunrise/sunset, etc. |

> ⚠️ **A "stop" voice command is not supported.** To stop a moving blind, use **■ Stop** in the EasyRoll app or on the remote.

---

## What You'll Need

- An account with **your blind registered in the EasyRoll app** ([Register Device](../easyroll/02_앱_등록하기.md) completed)
- The **Google Home app** installed on your smartphone + signed in with your Google account
- The blind **connected to the internet (home Wi-Fi)** — the Google Home integration works through the EasyRoll server
- For accurate position (%) commands, the blind's **upper/lower limits** must be set ([2-1. Upper/Lower Limit Setup](../easyroll/02-1_상하단_위치_설정.md))

## Connecting (One-Time Setup)

1. **Google Home app** → **[＋]** at the top right → **[＋ Device]**
2. At the bottom of the QR code screen, tap **[＋ Add another way]** → under **Search for brand or device**, search for **"EasyRoll"**
3. **Log in with your EasyRoll account** → allow the connection
4. When your registered blinds appear in Google Home, **assign them to a room** (e.g. Living Room) and you're done!

> 💡 **All blinds registered in the EasyRoll app are added to Google Home at once.** If a blind you registered later doesn't show up, say **"Hey Google, sync my devices"** or refresh the link in the Google Home app.
> 💡 Give the blind a **short name** (e.g. "Living Room Blind") so voice commands are recognized easily. You can rename it in the device settings of the Google Home app.

## How You Can Use It

| Method | Example |
|---|---|
| **Voice commands** | "Hey Google, open the living room blind" / "Close the blind" / "Open the blind halfway" / "Open the blind to 30%" / "Is the living room blind open?" |
| **By room** | With several blinds in one room, "Hey Google, close all the living room blinds" moves them **all at once** |
| **Google Home app · Nest Hub** | Open/close and position slider on the device card |
| **Routines** | Open the blinds in a "Good morning" routine, close them in a "Good night" routine, close all when leaving home, follow sunrise/sunset times |

## When Things Don't Work

| Symptom | Solution |
|---|---|
| EasyRoll doesn't appear in search | Update the Google Home app to the latest version and try again |
| Connected but the device doesn't show | First check in the EasyRoll app that the blind is properly registered and online → "Hey Google, sync my devices" |
| Voice commands don't respond | Check the device name (the name you call it in Google Home) — use a short, easy-to-say name that doesn't clash with a room name |
| "Set the blind to 50%" doesn't work | The blind's **upper/lower limits** aren't set → [2-1. Upper/Lower Limit Setup](../easyroll/02-1_상하단_위치_설정.md) |
| Position % looks reversed | Google Home uses **100% = fully open**. This is not a fault |
| It doesn't operate | Check that the blind is online ([FAQ](../easyroll/06_문제해결_FAQ.md)) → disconnect the link and reconnect |
