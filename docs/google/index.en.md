# Google Home Integration

Connect your EasyRoll blind to **Google Home** to operate it by **"Hey Google" voice** from your Google Nest speaker or Android phone.

---

## What You'll Need

- An account with **your blind registered in the EasyRoll app** ([Register Device](../easyroll/02_앱_등록하기.md) completed)
- The **Google Home app** installed on your smartphone + signed in with your Google account

## Connecting (One-Time Setup)

1. **Google Home app** → **[＋] button** in the top right → **[＋ Device]**
2. At the bottom of the QR code scan screen, tap **[＋ Add another way]** → search for **"EasyRoll"** under **Search for a brand or device**
3. **Log in with your EasyRoll account** → allow the connection
4. Once your registered blinds appear in Google Home, **assign them to a room (living room, etc.) and you're done!**

## How You Can Use It

| Method | Example |
|---|---|
| **Voice commands** | "Hey Google, open the living room blind" / "Close the blind" / "Set the blind to 50%" |
| **Google Home app** | Open/close and adjust position from the device card |
| **Routines** | Open the blinds in a "Good morning" routine, close them in a bedtime routine, etc. |

## When Things Don't Work

| Symptom | Solution |
|---|---|
| EasyRoll doesn't appear in search | Update the Google Home app to the latest version and try again |
| Connected but the device doesn't show | First check in the EasyRoll app that the blind is properly registered and online |
| Voice commands don't respond | Check the device name (the name you call it in Google Home) — we recommend a short, easy-to-say name |
| It doesn't operate | Check that the blind is online ([FAQ](../easyroll/06_문제해결_FAQ.md)) → disconnect the link and reconnect |
